Appendix · Judges’ questions

Answers to have ready

Is the level accurate?

It’s scoped: “strong at Medium on 12 questions”, never a global label. Thin evidence is flagged.

Where do weights come from?

Share of a role’s job posts naming the skill. Samples are synthetic today; real postings are next.

Why no AI grading?

Rules are explainable and free to run. AI fits reading job posts, as SkillBridge does with TF-IDF.

Can students cheat?

It’s for self-improvement, not certification. Shuffled options and a stored deadline keep it fair.

How does it scale?

Static files on any host: no server cost per user. Accounts come with the college pilot.

What about privacy?

Nothing leaves the browser. No login, no personal data collected.

Longer answers if a judge pushes: Accuracy: every question has one correct option and an explanation; topics with a single question are marked “needs more evidence”, and one missed Hard question becomes a practice suggestion. Weights: SkillBridge counts how many of a role’s job posts mention each skill (SQL appears in 9 of 10 Data Analyst samples, so weight 0.9). We are transparent in the app that the samples are synthetic. AI: the original SkillBridge prototype uses keyword matching plus TF-IDF similarity to extract skills from job descriptions; scores stay rule-based so every number can be explained. Monetisation, if asked: free for learners; colleges pay for the placement-cell dashboard and batch reports.
