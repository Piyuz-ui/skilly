Step 5 · The SkillBridge engine

Every priority is a sum you can check

Priority = gap × weight
Readiness = weighted share of target levels met
Weeks = hours (gap × hours per level) ÷ weekly time
  • Weight = share of a role’s job posts that name the skill.
  • A prerequisite with a gap is scheduled first (HTML & CSS before JavaScript).
